# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Scrum Master

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies, remove impediments, and coach teams on agile best practices. They work closely with Project Managers and Developers to maintain smooth delivery cadence and foster a culture of continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, sprint reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help remove impediments blocking the delivery team
- Coach the team on agile principles and practices
- Protect team focus and minimize interruptions
- Track sprint metrics and team velocity
- Escalate systemic blockers to Project Manager or Product Lead

### Goals
- Maintain consistent sprint rhythm and team velocity
- Increase team autonomy and self-organization
- Foster psychological safety and continuous improvement
- Reduce cycle time through process optimization

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Project Manager**: Coordinates on timeline, escalations, and cross-team dependencies
- **Developers**: Removes day-to-day impediments and facilitates ceremonies
- **Product Manager**: Aligns on sprint goals and backlog priorities
- **QA/Testing**: Ensures quality is built into sprint definition of done

### Typical Communication
- Daily standup facilitation and blocker tracking
- Sprint retrospectives and action item follow-up
- Agile metrics dashboards and team velocity reports
- One-on-ones with team members on performance and growth

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers lead design discovery, user research, and usability validation. They collaborate with Product Managers on user journeys and acceptance criteria, deliver assets and specifications for Developers, and review features pre-release to ensure excellent user experience.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and discovery to inform product decisions
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specs
- Define user journeys and accessibility standards
- Collaborate with Product Managers on acceptance criteria
- Deliver design systems, component libraries, and detailed specs to Developers
- Perform usability testing and user acceptance validation
- Review implemented features to ensure design fidelity and UX quality

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, user-centered product experiences
- Reduce user friction and support task completion
- Maintain design consistency across the product
- Advocate for user needs throughout the project lifecycle

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Manager**: Partners on user insights, prioritization, and success metrics
- **Developers**: Provides specs, participates in design reviews, answers implementation questions
- **QA/Testing**: Supports user acceptance testing and usability validation
- **Project Manager**: Aligns on design milestones and timeline

### Typical Communication
- Design review sessions with engineering and product
- User research findings and design documentation
- Design system updates and component specifications
- Feature walkthroughs and UAT support

---

## Technical Writer

### Role Summary
Technical Writers create and maintain documentation for features, APIs, and user-facing guides. They ensure documentation is kept up to date as part of Definition of Done and work with Developers, Product Managers, and QA to clarify intended usage and document gaps.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain user guides, API documentation, and technical guides
- Define documentation requirements as part of acceptance criteria
- Work with Developers to understand feature details and intended usage
- Review code and pull requests for documentation completeness
- Ensure documentation is kept current with product changes
- Improve documentation based on user feedback and support tickets
- Maintain style guides and documentation templates

### Goals
- Reduce support burden through clear, accessible documentation
- Enable users to self-serve and accomplish tasks independently
- Maintain documentation quality and accuracy across releases
- Support product adoption and user satisfaction

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborates on feature documentation and API specs
- **Product Manager**: Aligns on feature messaging and user guides
- **QA/Testing**: Reviews documentation for completeness and accuracy
- **Project Manager**: Ensures documentation is included in Definition of Done

### Typical Communication
- Documentation pull requests and review comments
- API and feature specification documents
- Release notes and migration guides
- Documentation-related stories in sprint planning

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers plan, coordinate, and oversee releases across staging and production environments. They work with Project Managers, Developers, QA, and Product teams to ensure release quality, manage communication, and coordinate rollback procedures if needed.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and schedule releases (timing, coordination, communication)
- Create and maintain release checklists and deployment procedures
- Coordinate pre-release activities (testing, documentation, release notes)
- Manage release communication to stakeholders and support teams
- Oversee deployment to staging and production environments
- Perform or coordinate post-deployment verification and smoke testing
- Manage rollback procedures and incident communication if issues arise
- Maintain release documentation and deployment runbooks

### Goals
- Minimize release risk and production incidents
- Ensure consistent, repeatable deployment processes
- Reduce time-to-market while maintaining quality
- Maintain transparency and clear communication during releases

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Project Manager**: Aligns on release timing, milestones, and stakeholder updates
- **Developers**: Coordinates merge and deployment timing, addresses deployment issues
- **QA/Testing**: Ensures quality gates are met before release, coordinates smoke testing
- **Product Manager**: Communicates release content, features, and timing to customers
- **Operations/Infrastructure**: Coordinates infrastructure needs and deployment tooling

### Typical Communication
- Release planning meetings and schedules
- Release notes and deployment documentation
- Pre-release and post-deployment status updates
- Incident and rollback communication if needed
